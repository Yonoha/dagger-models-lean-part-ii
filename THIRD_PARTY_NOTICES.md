# Dependencies and attribution

Original companion code and documentation are distributed under Apache-2.0;
see `LICENSE`. Existing file headers and attribution in the copied Part II
development sources are preserved.

The Part I companion is used as a pinned Git dependency, not copied into this
repository. Its [third-party notices](https://github.com/Yonoha/dagger-models-lean-part-i/blob/9ee62295a79ef61269259e27216afc0292458931/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
and the applicable upstream terms continue to govern its own reused sources.
In particular, the Part I notices distinguish observed file-level Apache
headers from repository-wide licensing that was not confirmed. This Part II
publication creates no new upstream license grant or permission statement.

Mathlib and its transitive packages retain their respective licenses and
copyright notices. Their exact revisions are recorded in `lake-manifest.json`.
Model-category interfaces, factorization, lifting, groupoid completion, nerve,
localization and simplicial infrastructure are reused from those dependencies
and the existing Part I development. The theorem catalogue does not claim
those foundations as new mathematics.
