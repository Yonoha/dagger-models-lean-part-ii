\subsection*{Notation and correspondence}

The principal notation follows Part II.
The category $\prism$ is the simplex category,
$\prism_{\rev}$ includes reversal maps,
and $\prism_{\dag}$ is the actual free-dagger-simplex index.
The resolution presentation is
$\sSpace^{\dag\vee}=\Fun(\prism^{\mathrm{op}},\sSet^{\dag})$;
the reversal presentation is
$\sSpace^{\dag}_{\rev}=\Fun(\prism_{\rev}^{\mathrm{op}},\sSet)$.
The external tensor and $\Map(A,W)$ in the resolution presentation differ from
the pointwise tensor and literal simplicial mapping space
$\underline{\Map}(A,W)$ in the reversal presentation.
The code pointers disambiguate every construction.
Specific recognition or reflection entries retain their explicit hypotheses;
contextual manuscript labels do not assert that every clause of a labelled
manuscript theorem has been formalized.
