\section{Frames, realization, density and recognition}

The results use the actual reversal-natural frames and specified comparison maps. Sufficient recognition criteria and reflection theorems retain their explicit hypotheses; a contextual manuscript label is not a claim to formalize every clause of that labelled assertion.

\begin{resultblock}
\begin{proposition}[The actual two-index dagger Joyal--Tierney realization]
\label{catalogue:F1}
For a reversal-compatible simplicial space $W$ at value universe zero, the Joyal--Tierney coend $t_!(\operatorname{res}W)$ carries the manuscript's prescribed dagger.
On a representative $[\alpha,\xi,w]$, this dagger reverses the simplex coordinate, inverts the groupoid-completed coordinate, and applies horizontal reversal to $w$.
The construction is natural in $W$, involutive, and fixes every vertex.
Its underlying simplicial set is exactly the ordinary two-index realization.
The constructed functor has a genuine right adjoint on the original dagger diagram category.
\end{proposition}

\begin{resultblock}
\begin{proposition}[Ordered-fiber Rezk recognition]
\label{catalogue:F13}
Let $f:W\to Z$ be a map between injectively fibrant reversal-compatible simplicial spaces satisfying the unitarily complete Segal condition.
If $f$ is surjective on $\pi_0$ of the zero row and induces weak equivalences on every strict ordered mapping fiber over pairs of vertices of $W$, then $f$ is an injective weak equivalence.
This is a proved criterion with its hypotheses displayed.
The separate implications from decorated evaluator weakness or fixed-target comma contractibility to Theorem C remain conditional statements when used through this route.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Conditional reflection by the zero-vertex functor]
\label{catalogue:F14}
Assume that the original constant--vertex and zero-vertex adjunction is Quillen for the injective Segal model.
Under that explicit premise, the zero-vertex right adjoint reflects weak equivalences between fibrant objects:
if its image of $f:Z\to Z'$ is a dagger Joyal weak equivalence, then $f$ is a weak equivalence in the injective Segal model.
The proof first establishes a Kan weak equivalence in every reversal-simplex row by comparing frame mapping targets.
This declaration does not itself prove that the constant--vertex adjunction is Quillen.
\end{proposition}
