\section{Ordinary completion and the final raw ordered unit}

These eight previously checked groups close the original raw ordered-unit input without imposing completeness on the original Segal source. Their ordinary constructions are auxiliary inputs to the dagger comparison, with the exact endpoint calibration retained.

\begin{resultblock}
\begin{proposition}[Ordinary Joyal--Tierney comparison]
\label{catalogue:C1}
The actual ordinary two-index realization and native-nerve adjunction is a
Quillen equivalence from the specified ordinary complete-Segal-space model
to the original Joyal model on simplicial sets.
The realization and right adjoint are the fixed functors of the development.

\begin{proposition}[The corrected precompletion unit]
\label{catalogue:C2}

the unit from $W$ to its specified corrected vertical precompletion is a weak

\begin{resultblock}
\begin{proposition}[Complete Segal replacement]
\label{catalogue:C3}
Let $W$ be an injectively fibrant ordinary Segal space.
Let $R$ be injectively fibrant and let
$\operatorname{precompletion}(W)\to R$ be rowwise weak.
Then $R$ satisfies both the Segal condition and the completeness condition.
The specified coherent comparison into this same $R$ is a Kan--Quillen weak
equivalence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{resultblock}
\begin{proposition}[Extension through the chosen completion]
\label{catalogue:C5}
Let $W$ be an injectively fibrant ordinary Segal space and let $Z$ be fibrant
in the ordinary complete-Segal-space model.
Every map $W\to Z$ extends through the chosen completion unit.
If the given map is a complete-Segal-space weak equivalence,
the extension is weak as well,
with its composite equal to the given map.
\end{proposition}
